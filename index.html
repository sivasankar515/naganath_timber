Our Legacy

Building Tamil Nadu,
One Roof at a Time

Established in 1958, our shop has built a long-standing reputation for quality and craftsmanship. Originally founded under a different name, we rebranded in 1990 as S.Naganath Timber to reflect our growth and expanded offerings.

We specialise in high-quality clay tiles, timber, cement sheets, roofing materials, lime, coir rope and more. With decades of experience, we pride ourselves on excellence in durability and aesthetics.

Located in Paramakudi, Ramanathapuram District, we serve architects, builders, and homeowners across Tamil Nadu with pride and passion.
