About Us

Established in 1958, our shop has a long-standing reputation for quality and craftsmanship. Originally founded under a different name, we rebranded in 1990 as S.Naganath Timber to reflect our growth and expanded expertise in the timber and clay tile industry.

S.Naganath Timber has been a trusted name in the timber and clay tile industry. We specialize in high-quality clay tiles and wood. With decades of experience, we pride ourselves on delivering excellence in durability and aesthetics.

Our mission is to provide customers with eco-friendly, long-lasting, and aesthetically pleasing solutions for homes and businesses. We source the finest materials and employ skilled artisans to ensure every product meets our rigorous quality standards.

Whether you're looking for traditional Kerala clay tiles, polished clay tiles, or bespoke wood, we have a wide range of products to suit your needs. Our commitment to customer satisfaction and quality has made us a preferred choice for architects, builders, and homeowners alike.
